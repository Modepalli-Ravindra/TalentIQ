# 01 Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Executive Summary
TalentIQ AI is an enterprise-grade hiring intelligence platform built to solve inefficiency, candidate drop-off, and screening overhead in modern technical recruitment.

## 2. Core Personas & Roles

### 2.1 Candidate
- **Goal**: Find high-fit engineering/product roles, evaluate readiness, and track applications with full transparency.
- **Pain Point**: Lack of feedback on why resumes are rejected; submitting to mismatched roles.

### 2.2 Recruiter / Hiring Manager
- **Goal**: Rapidly shortlist top talent, eliminate manual resume screening, and maintain high hiring velocity.
- **Pain Point**: Drowning in hundreds of unqualified applications per job post.

### 2.3 System Administrator
- **Goal**: Manage organization access, oversee subscription tiers, monitor audit trails, and maintain platform stability.
